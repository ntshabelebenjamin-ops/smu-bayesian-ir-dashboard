# Conference demonstration run sheet

## 1. Open with the strategic question
How can Institutional Research connect student success, equity and financial sustainability?

## 2. Show the data
649 unique FTEN students; 246 with observed HEMIS outcomes; 229 at/above 75% and 17 below 75%.

## 3. Show preprocessing
Emphasise reproducibility, no outcome imputation, leakage prevention and transparent variable engineering.

## 4. Explain the variable framework
Schooling context, language transition, household resources, first-generation context, NSFAS/funding, programme choice, time/access and psychosocial transition.

## 5. Show the Bayesian posterior
Explain odds ratios and credible intervals in plain language. Do not equate association with causation.

## 6. Show validation
This is the critical IR moment: apparent patterns do not automatically generalise. The small below-threshold group limits predictive performance.

## 7. Executive implication
Use evidence to design and evaluate support; do not use structural disadvantage as an exclusionary risk label.

## 8. Close with future research
SMU contextual model → CPUT matric/academic model → cross-institutional comparison → future hierarchical Bayesian model.
